# Manifesto: From Victim to Architect

> _"I started my journey as a victim of the digital age. I intend to continue it as one of its architects."_
>
> \
> My journey into cybersecurity didn't begin in a classroom or a boot camp. It began at home, right after my 10th-grade exams in 2020.
>
> I wasn't a prodigy; I was a victim. I fell prey to a cyberattack \[checkout my first cyber attack for details] that compromised my personal data and attempted to blackmail me. At 14, the feeling of vulnerability was overwhelming. But instead of shutting down the computer in fear, I felt a spark of defiance.
>
> <a class="button secondary">+1</a>
>
> I wanted to understand _how_ it happened. I wanted to know how to stop it from happening again - to me, or anyone else.
>
> <a class="button secondary"></a>
>
> ### **The Shift to Building**
>
> For the last few years, I have taught myself to code, not just to build apps, but to understand the logic of the systems I wanted to defend. I realized that the status quo of defense is too slow. We wait for an attack, analyze it manually, and write a rule. The attackers are automated; why aren't we?.
>
> <a class="button secondary"></a>
>
> This drove me to build SentinelX, a hybrid engine that combines the reliability of YARA with the reasoning of Generative AI. It represents my philosophy: The future of security isn't just about working harder; it's about building systems that think faster..
>
> <a class="button secondary">+1</a>
>
> #### The Force Multiplier
>
> I learned early on that a solo defender can protect a server, but only a culture of security can protect an ecosystem. This is why I founded RootX, a technical community where I mentor students to debug code, build CTF challenges, and simulate Red Team scenarios.
>
> <a class="button secondary">+1</a>
>
> I am no longer that 14-year-old victim. I am a Security Researcher, and I am here to build the architecture of the future.
